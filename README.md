# A City-Sized Jellyfish

Entry for make.fun bounty [ai-excited-01](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01) — "Make the AI judge excited".

A colossal glowing jellyfish, the size of a city, drifts above a small coastal
town at dusk. Its dome burns magenta and gold; luminous tentacles trail toward
the rooftops while a tiny fishing boat sails out to meet it. The impossible
scale — a creature bigger than the town, and a boat smaller than its shadow —
is the awe; the glowing dome reads clearly even at thumbnail size.

`main.ts` copies `entry.jpg` to `entry.png` in the output directory
(`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `/output`). Deterministic, no network.
